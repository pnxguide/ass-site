The three roles

Each studio has three members: a Developer, a Designer and a Business member. You register your studio in class, on one form signed by all three.

Developer

Architecture, agent-driven implementation, code review, testing, deployment

Steers agents to build, test, and ship the system

Designer

Users, user flows, interface and interaction design, usability evidence

Steers agents to generate and iterate interfaces and flows

Business

Problem selection, value proposition, market and cost reasoning, pitch narrative

Steers agents for market and cost analysis, data, and pitch materials
