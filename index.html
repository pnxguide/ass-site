Course Description

As coding agents become increasingly capable, they enable rapid construction of greenfield software for prototyping and business proofing. Effective use of these agents, however, requires sufficient software knowledge to specify, steer, review, and improve their work while controlling time and cost.

In this course, students form mini software studios and construct projects in web, mobile, data mining, games and simulations, agentic applications, and production software systems. Students learn the underlying components well enough to direct coding agents wisely, validate technical results, and professionally pitch their prototypes.
